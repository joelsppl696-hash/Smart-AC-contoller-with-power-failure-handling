# IOT-PROJECT-GROUP-3
---
### OVERVIEW
Smart AC Controller is an ESP32-based intelligent automation system designed to reduce unnecessary electricity consumption in educational institutions. The system automatically turns OFF air conditioners when rooms remain vacant, after office hours, and after power restoration if no occupants are detected. Every event is recorded to an SD card for future energy auditing.
### PROBLEM STATEMENT 
Modern educational institutions consume a significant amount of electrical energy through air conditioning systems. However, a considerable portion of this energy is wasted due to the lack of intelligent control and monitoring. In many classrooms and staff rooms, air conditioners continue running even after occupants leave because users forget to switch them OFF. Additionally, during power outages, most AC units automatically resume operation when electricity is restored, as they retain their previous ON state. This results in unnecessary energy consumption in unoccupied rooms.
Another major challenge is the absence of an event logging system to verify whether energy-saving practices are being followed. Facility administrators have no reliable record of AC usage, making it difficult to perform energy audits or identify instances of power wastage.
To address these issues, this project proposes an ESP32-based Smart AC Controller with Power Failure Handling. The system monitors room occupancy using a PIR motion sensor and automatically switches OFF the AC after a configurable period of inactivity. It also verifies room occupancy after office hours and ensures that the AC remains OFF after power restoration if the room is empty. A DS3231 Real-Time Clock (RTC) provides accurate timestamps, while all system events—including AC ON/OFF operations, motion timeout, office-hour shutdown, power failure, and power restoration—are recorded in a CSV file on an SD card. During power outages, the ESP32 is powered by a power bank, allowing uninterrupted event logging and reliable detection of power restoration. This solution enhances energy efficiency, reduces unnecessary electricity consumption, and provides a transparent audit trail for institutional energy management.
